1. Executive architecture vision

Release 1 is a controlled, case-centred due-diligence capability for the first 20 eligible new private-equity opportunities. It preserves authoritative source systems and human investment authority while adding governed evidence ingestion, provenance, assistive analysis, citation, mandatory review, independent validation and auditable operations.

Business outcome

Measure formal case opening to an investment-committee-ready draft and support a median no greater than three weeks without reducing quality.

Authority boundary

AI remains assistive. Deal professionals validate, Legal and Compliance approve applicable conclusions, and the Investment Committee retains every investment decision.

Evidence boundary

Only governed, owner-bound and permitted evidence reaches analysis; material claims retain provenance and citations.

Phase boundary

ABBs and logical controls only. AFF-3 selects candidate Azure SBBs after Phase 2 approval.

Scope and tailoring

This iteration combines TOGAF Phases A–D at target depth. Baseline statements are limited to approved evidence. Opportunities, migration detail, implementation, deployment and runtime testing are deferred. Requirements Management remains continuous.

These design rules derive from approved requirements and add no independent requirement.

Evidence before assertionEvery material claim traces to accessible governed evidence.Provenance and citation are processing gates.
Sources stay authoritativeIntegration is read-only or controlled ingestion.No AI-generated source write-back.
Govern at admissionOwner, licence, purpose, classification and quality precede use.Failed gates quarantine or reject evidence.
Minimise and containUse necessary data within approved location and transfer boundaries.Rules cover data, logs, inputs and outputs.

Fail closedMissing approval or evidence blocks progression.No silent bypass.
Operations produce evidenceBusiness, security and service events are observable and correlated.Metrics, logs, traces and incidents support assurance.

7. Logical Technology, Security, Operations and Resilience

Trust

Explicit user and workload identity, controlled purpose, least privilege, time-bound privileged elevation and governed emergency access.

Policy

Logically common decisions with enforcement at admission, access, analysis, review and transfer boundaries.

Isolation

Separate user, application, integration, data and operations trust zones; separate development, test and production.

Operations

Correlated metrics, logs and traces; 8x5 support; critical-alert assignment and incident evidence.

Resilience

Recoverable case, evidence, policy and audit state supporting 99.9% business-hours availability, RTO ≤4h and RPO ≤1h.

Performance

Separate logical interactive and document-pack work to support p95 <5s and typical pack ≤30m.
