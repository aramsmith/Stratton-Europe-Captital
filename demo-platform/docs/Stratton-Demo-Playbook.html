The governed demo flow

The demo separates supporting AI work from investment authority. Evidence must be admitted, analysis must be grounded and cited, human reviews are version-bound, and the final output remains a draft committee pack.

Admit evidence

Four synthetic Project Danube documents enter the governed evidence set.

Create bundle

Phase 5 authorizes an immutable, tenant-scoped analysis bundle.

Run analysis

The BFF performs supporting retrieval and model work with citation checks.

Human review

Deal, Legal, and Compliance approvals bind to the exact authoritative version.

Prepare draft

A draft-only committee pack is prepared. No investment decision is issued.

Submit pack

A human operator records delivery to the committee and completes the demo.

Authority boundary: the system records that a governed pack was submitted to the committee. It never records or implies an investment approval.

Step-by-step operator manual

Follow these steps in order. Each instruction names the exact control to click, why the action matters, and what must appear before continuing.

1

Sign in to the live application

Click: Sign in with Microsoft, then select the approved aram@azurelab.nl account.

Why: every admission, finding disposition, review, and audit event must be attributable to an authenticated operator.

Continue when: the header shows Aram Smith, initials AS, and Authenticated user.

2

Start a fresh governed cycle

Click: Start new analysis cycle in the Analytical task panel when it is shown. Alternatively, click Reset case in the upper-right header, then Confirm reset.

Why: reset creates a new analysis-cycle identity. This allows a repeatable demonstration without changing or overwriting prior immutable bundles and human decisions.

Continue when: the case stage is INTAKE, all four evidence items are QUARANTINED, and no findings are shown.

3

Open the evidence workspace

Click: AI Deal Workbench in the left navigation.

Why: this workspace governs the transition from quarantined source material to evidence that is eligible for analysis.

Continue when: the Evidence admission table and Analytical task panel are visible.

4

Admit all four evidence sources

Select: leave all four evidence checkboxes selected, or use Select all available evidence. Then click Admit selected evidence (4) once.

Why: analysis is permitted to retrieve only evidence with approved ownership, licence, provenance, tenant, case, and admission state.

Continue when: every row shows ADMITTED and its admission button is disabled.

5

Define the governed analysis request

Select: 3. Investment-thesis challenge in Analysis task. The choices are ordered from grounded analysis, through cross-document comparison, to investment-thesis challenge. In Question, use Challenge management EBITDA quality.

Why: the task class determines the approved Luna, Terra, or Sol route, while the exact question becomes part of the immutable request fingerprint.

Continue when: the task and question are visible and Run investment-thesis challenge is enabled.

6

Run cited analysis

Click: Run investment-thesis challenge.

Why: Phase 5 authorizes a tenant-scoped immutable bundle before the BFF performs retrieval and model work. The route fails closed rather than silently falling back.

Continue when: three finding cards appear and the latest governed Phase 5 request shows its run ID, task class, question, and admitted evidence set.

7

Inspect the supporting citations

Click: Open citation on each finding, including the page 42 citation for Adjusted EBITDA quality.

Why: a material claim must be traceable to admitted source evidence. This demonstrates that the model output is reviewable rather than accepted as an unsupported assertion.

Continue when: the citation detail and side-by-side source comparison show the source locator, excerpt, and evidence identity.

8

Record human finding dispositions

Click: Accept finding on Adjusted EBITDA quality, Customer concentration, and Permit transfer readiness.

Why: AI findings remain drafts until a human explicitly accepts, edits, challenges, or rejects them. Acceptance creates the governed content used by specialist reviews.

Continue when: all three findings show ACCEPTED.

9

Create mandatory security evidence

Click: Governance & Assurance Console, select the Security & audit tab, then click Run security gate checks.

Why: the committee pack requires current, version-bound evidence for twelve deterministic controls covering injection, retrieval, routing, fallback, and autonomous authority.

Continue when: gates CC002-R2-SEC-GATE-001 through 012 all show PASS.

10

Open the specialist review workspace

Click: Investment Decision Room in the left navigation.

Why: this workspace separates human investment review from AI-supported evidence and analysis.

Continue when: the accepted material claims and Required specialist reviews are visible.

11

Approve the Deal review

Enter: a concise rationale in Deal review rationale, then click Approve Deal review.

Why: the Deal reviewer confirms that the accepted commercial finding is suitable for the current committee-preparation version.

Continue when: the Deal review shows APPROVED.

12

Approve the Legal review

Enter: a rationale in Legal review rationale, then click Approve Legal review.

Why: Legal confirms the permit and transaction-related claim against the exact accepted finding version.

Continue when: the Legal review shows APPROVED.

13

Approve the Compliance review

Enter: a rationale in Compliance review rationale, then click Approve Compliance review.

Why: Compliance confirms that the governed evidence and current claim version satisfy the required control boundary.

Continue when: Deal, Legal, and Compliance all show APPROVED.

14

Verify governance lineage and routing

Click: Governance & Assurance Console, then inspect Lineage, Policy decisions, and Model routes.

Why: these tabs prove the relationship between admitted evidence, the immutable bundle, model routing, human decisions, and the prepared draft.

Continue when: the current authoritative version is traceable and no silent fallback or cross-case route is present.

15

Confirm the audit-ready outcome

Click: the Security & audit tab again and review Audit export preview.

Why: audit readiness proves that lineage, policy decisions, model routes, security evidence, and specialist reviews are complete before the pack is prepared.

Continue when: the export shows READY, all twelve gates show PASS, and Internal Audit verdict: Not issued remains visible.

16

Prepare the draft committee pack

Click: Investment Decision Room, then Prepare committee pack.

Why: this assembles only accepted findings with current specialist approvals and current security-gate evidence into a draft recommendation.

Continue when: the stage becomes COMMITTEE_PREPARATION and Submit to committee becomes enabled.

17

Submit the governed committee pack

Click: Submit to committee.

Why: this human-only action records delivery of the prepared package without creating an investment approval.

Finish when: the page confirms Committee pack submitted successfully. The Stratton demo is complete.

Deployed Azure architecture

The application is deployed in Sweden Central with governed Azure OpenAI routes in West Europe. Workload communication uses managed identities, private data-plane access, and digest-pinned images.

1

Browser and identity

The public web Container App authenticates through Microsoft Entra and acquires delegated BFF access without storing a secret.

2

Governed BFF

The BFF enforces delegated authorization, scenario policy, tenant/case boundaries, citations, review currentness, and committee-preparation rules.

3

Phase 5 authority

The private Phase 5 API authorizes immutable analysis bundles and validates signed BFF workload tokens before accepting completion.

4

Private data plane

Azure SQL, Blob Storage, Service Bus, and Search use managed identity and private network paths; Search evidence is bounded for model quota safety.

5

Governed AI routes

Luna, Terra, and Sol bind to approved Azure OpenAI deployments using API version 2025-04-01-preview, explicit output limits, and route evidence.
