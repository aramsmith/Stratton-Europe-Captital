## Business Challenge

A Vienna-based private equity fund, called ‘Stratton Europe Capital’ with €18 billion AUM managing
34 portfolio companies across Central Europe faces:

- Due diligence processes requiring 12 weeks and consuming 60% of deal team capacity
- Portfolio company performance monitoring relying on quarterly manual reporting with no real-time signals
- ESG reporting obligations under EU SFDR requiring data from 34 portfolio companies in inconsistent formats
- Deal sourcing dependent on personal networks with no systematic market intelligence capability
- Regulatory reporting for Luxembourg-based fund vehicles requiring AIFMD compliance documentation

## Transformation Objective

Deploy a sovereign investment intelligence platform that accelerates due diligence, enables real-time
portfolio monitoring, automates ESG reporting, and enhances deal sourcing through AI-driven market
intelligence.

- Current solution is a legacy on prem.
- Solution must land in the Azure Sovereign Landing zone
