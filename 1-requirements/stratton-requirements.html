Executive position

Release 1 is a controlled production rollout for the first 20 eligible new private-equity opportunities. Within 12 months of programme approval, it must reduce median due diligence from 12 weeks to no more than 3 weeks, measured from formal case opening to an investment-committee-ready recommendation. The Chief Investment Officer owns the outcome.

AI is assistive: it may process governed evidence and prepare drafts, while deal professionals validate material outputs, Legal and Compliance approve applicable specialist conclusions, and the Investment Committee retains every investment decision.

Boundary: this requirements candidate contains no architecture, Azure service selection, deployment claim, runtime result or Phase 1 approval.

Regulatory boundary

GDPR applies to EU personal-data processing; SFDR applies where ESG output supports regulated disclosures; AIFMD applies where Luxembourg AIF vehicles use the workflow; and the EU AI Act provides the confirmed AI-governance framework without an unsupported high-risk classification.

DORA applies where Stratton or the relevant managing entity is an in-scope AIFM or other financial entity. General Counsel still owns the entity-specific applicability or exemption record. Until it exists, Release 1 must support DORA-aligned ICT risk, incident, resilience-test and provider-oversight evidence and must not claim formal DORA compliance.
