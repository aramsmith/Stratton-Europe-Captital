Security, data, AI and integration

Identity

Stratton-controlled Entra identities, Conditional Access, PIM, separate workload managed identities and Entra-only SQL. Internal Audit alone controls verdict writes.

Evidence

A mandatory envelope binds source, owner, timestamp, licence, purpose, classification, quality, hash and admission state. Incomplete evidence cannot reach analysis.

AI boundary

Regional private inference in the approved pair, draft output only, no autonomous tools, no fine-tuning and no foundation-model training with Stratton data. Global/DataZone deployments are denied.

Legal boundary

Location and transfer allow-lists have no production default. No DORA compliance or AI Act classification claim is made while evidence remains open.

Traceability and governed controls

All 31 Must requirements, 19 ABBs and AD-001 through AD-010 have concrete Azure mappings. Human authority, source authority, production isolation, no-training use, sovereignty, legal-jurisdiction controls and Internal Audit independence remain intact.

Seven controls carried in formal evidence
  • VAL-001 — entity-specific DORA evidence before a formal claim.
  • VAL-002 — business hours, typical pack and critical alerts before acceptance.
  • VAL-003 — eligibility, benchmark, field, severity and exception definitions before validation.
  • VAL-004 — exact source, schedule, volume and remediation mapping before production ingestion.
  • VAL-005 — approved locations and transfer process before production acceptance.
  • AFFB-RES-001 — AI Act role/use-case classification before production or a claim.
  • AFFB-RES-002 — official citations, dates and article mappings before formal representation.

Deployment approach and AFF-4 handoff

Plan one modular Bicep codebase: Citadel management groups/subscriptions → ALZ, AI-governance and sovereign policies → two regional hubs/central DNS → identities/management → workload spokes → regional workload services → assurance → diagnostics. Pin and review the Foundry Citadel source revision; it remains an adapted architecture, not a product dependency.

Patterns are confirmed. Exact tenant parent, subscription IDs, both region names, CIDRs/WAN/DNS, owners, retention, source register, measured caps, model/quota, provider evidence and assurance groups have no defaults. Deployment uses human-interactive Azure sign-in or a separately approved runtime managed identity. GitHub OIDC and stored credentials are prohibited.
